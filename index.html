In the order I traveled it. Interchange stations, drawn with a black ring, are where I changed lines.

  1. Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham Engineering

    B.Tech, Computer Science & Engineering

    India, graduated June 2022

    First Class with Distinction. Two lines start here. The engineering line is the degree. The field line is what I did alongside it: most of my college years went into leading projects outside the classroom.

  2. Live-in-Labs Field

    Project Lead

    Kanaka and Rajanga villages, Odisha. Jan 2020 to Jan 2022

    Wild elephants were raiding farmland in two villages. I led a multidisciplinary student team designing a way to warn people without harming the herd: buried geophones pick up footfall, an Arduino filters the signal, and speakers and floodlights steer the elephants away. We took it to prototype with a sector-by-sector field test plan.

  3. Sign language detection app Field

    Team Lead

    Funded through a Huts Lab humanitarian program

    A deep learning model, for mobile and web, that lets hearing- and speech-impaired people communicate without extra hardware. The idea came out of my lab and I got it selected for funding. I led the 3-person team and owned the sponsor relationship, with weekly progress reports to our funder.

  4. HyperVerge Engineering

    Integrations Engineer Intern

    Bengaluru. Jan to Jul 2022

    My first job sat between code and customers. I ran technical onboarding for 10+ enterprise clients from kickoff call to go-live, cleared 40+ integration issues through ServiceNow and Jira, and wrote proof-of-concept API scripts that got new accounts connected faster.

  5. Arcadia Engineering

    Software Engineer

    Chennai. Aug 2022 to Mar 2023

    I built regex and SQL extraction templates for high-volume utility bills, cutting manual effort by 56%. Two other pieces of work pointed to where I was heading: reworking the extraction workflow so it no longer depended on one person, and scripting productivity and Jira reports that showed the team where its workload actually sat.
